PDF Editor

Edit text, annotate, sign, fill forms, redact, reorder pages and more — all in your browser

Processed in your browser — nothing is uploaded

What you can do

  • Edit existing text — click a line of text, retype it. Find & replace across the whole document.
  • Annotate — highlight, underline, strike through, draw, add shapes, arrows, stamps and comment notes.
  • Sign & fill forms — draw, type or upload a signature; fill existing form fields or add new ones.
  • Redact — black out sensitive content and remove it permanently.
  • Organise pages — reorder by drag and drop, rotate, delete, duplicate, insert blank pages or pages from another PDF, crop.
  • Finish — headers, footers, page numbers, watermarks and document properties. Undo/redo everything.

Good to know

  • “Edit existing text” covers the original line and writes new text on top in a matching standard font — the original text still exists underneath. Use Redact when content must be gone for good.
  • Password-protected PDFs must be unlocked first (Unlock PDF). Scanned PDFs need OCR before their text can be edited.
  • Press ? inside the editor for keyboard shortcuts.

Frequently asked questions

Is PDF Editor free?

Yes. The free plan covers this tool with no sign-up required.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. This tool runs entirely in your browser, so your PDF never leaves your device.

Which file types are supported?

Supported: .pdf. Files up to 50 MB are supported on the free plan.
